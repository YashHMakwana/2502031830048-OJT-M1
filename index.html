OUR HERITAGE

Earthy, Robust & Unapologetically Spicy

Kathiyawad—the land of royals, saints, and brave hearts in western Gujarat—has a cuisine that mirrors its climate. Resilient crops like pearl millet (Bajra), high-intensity red garlic, and heavy dairy fats are crafted into deeply satisfying and fiery meals meant to sustain dryland farming.

At Rangilu Kathiyawad, we pay homage to this rustic history. We do not dilute the garlic, we do not compromise on the ghee, and we pat the rotlas by hand exactly the way it has been done in village courtyards for centuries.

  • Gondal Reshampatti garlic tempering
  • Sourced from organic co-op farms in Saurashtra
